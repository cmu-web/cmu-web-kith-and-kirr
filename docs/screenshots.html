A Tour of the Theme

Each screen of Grouper 7.9.1 as it ships, then with Kith & Kirr, with the same made-up groups and people. Select a screenshot to see it full size.

Home

The template's red roof, your site's name and the Grouper logo replace Grouper's header, and the side menu becomes the template's link list.

A Group's Members

The members table gets the Kilt's look: labeled filter fields, a More button for each row and the Kilt's pager.

A Folder

Breadcrumbs, the folder's heading and description, and its contents follow the Kilt.

A Person

A person's page keeps Grouper's fields and tabs, in the template's type and colors.

A Form

Labels sit in small capitals above square fields, in the template's type.

On a Phone

The header, the side menu and each table row stack to fit the screen; in a table, labels sit over their values and each row keeps its More button.
